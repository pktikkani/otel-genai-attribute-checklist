How to use this checklist

01

Choose the signal and operation that match the boundary you are instrumenting.

02

Apply the published span name and span kind where the source defines them.

03

Work down the rows in requirement order. Keep each source condition attached to its row.

04

Treat content-bearing opt-in fields carefully; the source warns that several may contain sensitive information.

05

Use Appendix A to look up every registry entry, including attributes not assigned to one checklist here.
